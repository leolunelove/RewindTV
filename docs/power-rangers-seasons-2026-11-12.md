## Acceptance rule and sources

Every addition has `OK` watch metadata, explicit embedding permission and 249 distinct declared territories. Missing declarations and known exclusions never qualify. Provider declarations can change; this is not a guarantee of worldwide playback.

Sources are the established Power Rangers Official channel, its authenticated Kids channel, and the authenticated HasTV Superheroes publisher. HasTV provenance was traced through exact primary publisher links: [Hasbro's corporate social directory](https://corporate.hasbro.com/en-us/social-media) → its Hasbro Games channel and curated product-demo publishers → [Hasbro](https://www.youtube.com/@hasbroOfficial/featured) → [HasTV Play](https://www.youtube.com/@HasTVPlay/featured) → [HasTV Superheroes](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCTdirWpnSlCD5rHjxKSyjmg). A badge or an official-looking title alone was not treated as ownership evidence.

Some official uploads are recent. Their exact upload dates are retained; the archive-age preference is not misrepresented as a three-year history for these uploads. No fan/archive sources were added.
