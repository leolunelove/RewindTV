The 4:3 and 16:9 controls move one shared aperture. The native picture stays contained; on portrait phones the controls follow the picture. A short landscape layout puts the remote to the right. The drawer and dialog transitions can reverse without leaving an interactive panel behind. Movie Night measures its card widths and gaps as the viewport changes, so a resize does not cancel the spin.

Shuffle (R) offers **This channel** or **All channels**. It chooses a show before picking an episode, skips ads and known playback failures, and prefers standalone episodes and recent-history alternatives. Picks start at the beginning. **Live TV** returns to the scheduled broadcast.
