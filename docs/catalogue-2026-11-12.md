## Availability and franchise coverage

The Power Rangers channel's intended scope is the entire franchise. **This release does not provide every season.** Its 23 accepted uploads are season-one Mighty Morphin episodes 3, 6, 9, 12, 14, 16, 17, 18, 21, 24, 25, 26, 28, 34, 36, 38, 45, 46, 47, 52, 53, 56 and 59, using the official publisher's numbering.

Every new upload requires `OK` watch metadata, explicit embedding permission and all 249 territories in the per-video availability declaration. Known exclusions and missing declarations are held out. This is a source-selection rule, not a worldwide playback guarantee.

The saved research queue contains **949 candidates**, including 868 Power Rangers uploads and alternate uploads. It is not an additions count:

- 317 received usable watch metadata: 87 declared all 249 territories; 230 declared regional exclusions.
- Of the 87, four remain held for runtime/completeness review: three MMPR uploads below 19 minutes and a 40-minute He-Man upload whose extent is unclear.
- 632 candidates still need usable metadata. Earlier rate-limited requests were deferred, not classified as broken.
- Within Power Rangers, 236 received usable metadata: 26 declared all 249 territories, of which 23 passed the runtime gate; 210 declared exclusions. Later-series samples and nine samples from the authenticated Kids channel declared exclusions. Unchecked later episodes are not assumed restricted.

Full-episode playlists were mapped across the original series, Alien Rangers, Zeo, Turbo, In Space, Lost Galaxy, Lightspeed Rescue, Time Force, Wild Force, Ninja Storm, Dino Thunder, S.P.D., Mystic Force, Operation Overdrive, Jungle Fury, RPM, Samurai, Super Samurai, Megaforce, Super Megaforce, Dino Charge and some Ninja Steel uploads. ## Playback evidence

**186 distinct videos passed actual muted start and near-end embedded playback on `https://tv.leolune.fun`**, including all 83 accepted additions, 101 other research candidates and two existing controls. Exact IDs, times, durations and origins are recorded in [the playback checkpoint](catalogue-playback-2026-11-12.json). Of the 949 research candidates, 765 have not received this public-origin playback check.

The probe requires an advancing clock at the beginning and again within the final 16 seconds. This does not establish uninterrupted full-runtime playback, every scene or complete credits. Official full-episode labels, runtime, episode identity and overlap checks are separate evidence.

Tests used this Mac's in-app browser and network; the egress country was not independently verified. No testing in 249 countries or physical iPhone/Safari certification is implied. Existing direct Archive streams were not rechecked in this content pass.

Localhost initially returned YouTube error 150 for 23 Power sources. Public-origin checks subsequently passed them. Those local errors were treated as origin-dependent/inconclusive, rather than evidence of deletion or a global embed block. Only the successful public-origin results qualify these additions.
